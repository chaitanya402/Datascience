## Getting Started

### First Launch
When you first open the app, you'll see an empty document list with a message: "No documents yet". Don't worry - let's create your first document!

### Main Screen Overview
The main screen displays:
- **Search Bar**: Find documents quickly by title
- **Document List**: Shows all your saved documents
- **Blue "+" Button**: Tap to create a new document

## Creating Your First Document

1. **Tap the blue "+" button** at the bottom right of the screen
2. **Enter a title** in the large text field at the top
3. **Start typing** your content in the main text area
4. **Tap the Save icon** (💾) in the top right when you're done

That's it! Your document is now saved and will appear in your document list.

## Editing Documents

### Opening a Document
From the main screen, simply **tap on any document** to open it for editing.

### Document Editor Features

#### Title Section
- Large, bold text field at the top
- Change the title anytime
- Required field (can't save without a title)

#### Formatting Toolbar
Located below the title, this toolbar gives you quick access to:
- **B** (Bold): Make text bold
- **I** (Italic): Italicize text
- **U** (Underline): Underline text
- **-** (Decrease): Make text smaller
- **+** (Increase): Make text larger

#### Content Area
- Spacious text area for your document
- Multiline input
- Scrollable for long documents
- Placeholder text helps you get started

#### Statistics Bar
At the bottom of the screen, you'll see:
- Word count
- Character count

These update in real-time as you type!

## Managing Documents

### Viewing All Documents
The main screen shows all your documents with:
- Document icon
- Title (large text)
- Last modified date
- Content preview (first 100 characters)

### Searching Documents
1. Tap the search bar at the top
2. Type any part of a document title
3. The list filters automatically

### Deleting Documents
1. Find the document you want to delete
2. Tap the **trash icon** (🗑️) on the right
3. Confirm the deletion when prompted

**Warning**: Deleted documents cannot be recovered!

### Sorting
Documents are automatically sorted by last modified date (newest first).

## Text Formatting

### Bold Text
1. Tap the **B** button in the toolbar
2. The button turns blue when active
3. All text you type will now be bold
4. Tap again to turn off

### Italic Text
1. Tap the **I** button in the toolbar
2. The button turns blue when active
3. All text you type will now be italic
4. Tap again to turn off

### Underline Text
1. Tap the **U** button in the toolbar
2. The button turns blue when active
3. All text you type will now be underlined
4. Tap again to turn off

### Changing Font Size
- Tap **-** to decrease font size (minimum: 10)
- Tap **+** to increase font size (maximum: 32)
- Default font size is 16

### Combining Formats
You can use multiple formats at once! Try:
- Bold + Italic
- Bold + Underline + Larger font
- Any combination you like

## Sharing Documents

### How to Share
1. Open the document you want to share
2. Tap the **share icon** (📤) in the top right
3. Choose your sharing method:
   - Email
   - Messages
   - Social media
   - Any app that accepts text

### What Gets Shared
The shared content includes:
- Document title
- Full document content
- Plain text format (formatting is removed)

## Tips and Tricks

### Quick Save
Always save your work! Tap the save icon frequently to avoid losing changes.

### Descriptive Titles
Use clear, descriptive titles for your documents. This makes searching easier!

### Regular Backups
While documents are saved locally, consider periodically sharing important documents to email or cloud storage as a backup.

### Long Documents
For very long documents, the app scrolls smoothly. Use the statistics bar to track your progress!

### Search Power
The search feature is case-insensitive, so "report" will find "Monthly Report".

### Empty State
If you delete all documents, you'll see the empty state again. Just tap the + button to start fresh!

## Keyboard Shortcuts (if available on your device)

Some devices support external keyboards with shortcuts:
- **Ctrl/Cmd + B**: Bold
- **Ctrl/Cmd + I**: Italic
- **Ctrl/Cmd + U**: Underline
- **Ctrl/Cmd + S**: Save

## Troubleshooting

### Documents Not Saving
- Make sure you've entered a title
- Check that you have storage space on your device
- Try restarting the app

### App Crashes
- Make sure you have the latest version
- Try restarting your device
- Check that you have enough free memory

### Formatting Not Working
- Make sure you're tapping the toolbar buttons
- Check that the buttons turn blue when active
- Try toggling the format off and on again

## Privacy & Storage

### Where Are Documents Stored?
Documents are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. They are:
- Private to your device
- Not backed up automatically
- Not synced across devices
- Deleted if you uninstall the app

### Security
- No internet connection required
- Documents never leave your device (unless you share them)
- No account or login needed
